How to Interpret These Results
Segmentation Mask

White pixels indicate regions identified as tumor tissue by the U-Net model. This gives the spatial extent and location of the lesion.

Grad-CAM Heatmap

Hot (red/yellow) areas are the regions that most influenced the classification decision. If the hotspot aligns with the tumor region, the model is attending to the right area.

Confidence Score

The probability assigned to the top predicted class. Higher values (>90%) indicate stronger model certainty. Always consult a qualified radiologist.
